# osrsiron

**https://kubnev.github.io/osrsiron/**

A simple website for following Old School RuneScape Ironman guides as a
checklist. Pick your account type at the top:

- **Ironman:** the **BRUHsailer** guide, plus **Ladlor's gear progression chart**
  under *Progression*.
- **Ultimate Ironman:** the OSRS Wiki's **Ultimate Ironman Walkthrough Guide
  to Prifddinas**.

Tick off steps as you go, search, highlight text, and collapse finished steps.
The guides load from their original sources each time you open the site, so
they stay up to date.

Your progress is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere and
there are no accounts. To keep a copy or move it to another device, use
**Backup progress** / **Restore backup** at the bottom of the page.
